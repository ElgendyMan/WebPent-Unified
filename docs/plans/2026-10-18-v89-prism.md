# v89 THE PRISM — Execution Plan (2026-10-18)

Scope: the complete Prism proposal — evidence taxonomy, classifier, scorer,
multi-signal integration, campaign ledger, pattern extraction, prior adjuster,
anomaly/value filters, rabbit-hole protector, brain integration, metrics,
dashboard+CLI, cold-start accelerator, silent-failure detector. 15 units,
5 waves, all advisory-only (PRISM-L-2), deterministic (PRISM-L-9),
no network, no PII, no LLM in decisions.

## Governing laws (implemented, not just written)

- PRISM-L-1 evidence is earned: no profile without all 5 dimensions, no
  score without components+rationale, no adjustment without reason.
- PRISM-L-2 advisory-only: Prism ranks/flags/suggests; it never blocks a
  finding's execution path. The compliance detector's "block" applies to
  Prism's own intake (an unscored finding gets no Prism score), never to
  the Brain's decisions.
- PRISM-L-3 structured learning: min samples 5, confidence floor 0.6,
  adjustment cap ±0.20, stale decay at 90 days.
- PRISM-L-4 no silent failures: every classify/score/adjust/decision can be
  audited; the detector flags violations with typed records.
- PRISM-L-9 deterministic: no randomness anywhere; ids from sha256 of
  stable content; ties broken by stable keys.
- PRISM-L-12 suite monotonic: ≥200 new tests, zero failures.
